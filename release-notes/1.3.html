Better answers when gestures stop, and two fixes for ways they could.

  • New Troubleshooting submenu. Restart Gesture Detection re-attaches the trackpad without relaunching. Debug Logging writes a detailed log to ~/Library/Logs/TabSwipe — every gesture, every sleep and wake, and a heartbeat once a minute — that you can attach to a bug report. Off by default; nothing is ever sent anywhere.
  • The Chrome target is re-checked whenever a gesture starts, so coming back to Chrome from the lock screen can no longer leave swipes with nowhere to go.
  • A trackpad restart after wake can no longer leave the swipe pacing timer stuck in the future, which would have silently blocked every swipe until relaunch.
