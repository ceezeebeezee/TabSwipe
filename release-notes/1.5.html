The fix for gestures that stop after sleep.

  • An external trackpad reconnects over Bluetooth a few seconds after your Mac wakes — after TabSwipe had already re-attached, and it never looked again. Now it re-attaches whenever a trackpad arrives or leaves, and double-checks every twenty seconds.
  • Chrome quitting, crashing or relaunching is handled explicitly: a swipe is never sent to a process that is gone, and a fresh Chrome is picked up as soon as it is in front.
